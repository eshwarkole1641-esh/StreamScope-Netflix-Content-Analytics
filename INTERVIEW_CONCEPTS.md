# Concepts to Explain in an Interview

- **Data profiling:** checking rows, columns, data types, missingness, and unique values.
- **Data cleaning:** handling duplicates, inconsistent text, and missing data without inventing unknown facts.
- **Type conversion:** parsing dates and converting year values to numeric fields.
- **Feature engineering:** deriving fields such as primary genre, primary country, and date-added month.
- **EDA:** summarizing and visualizing patterns before drawing conclusions.
- **Categorical analysis:** comparing counts for content type, countries, genres, and ratings.
- **Time-trend analysis:** inspecting records by release year or date-added year.
- **Validation:** rechecking missingness and types after transformations.
- **Dashboard:** enabling interactive filters, charts, search, and export.
- **Limitation:** catalogue counts alone cannot prove popularity, watch time, or audience preference.
